# **Races**

Races in the Home Brew mod have been largely standardized. All races have the same melee reach and a base movement speed of 9m/30ft. All changes listed here apply equally to the Player Character, Companions, and NPCs. 

## **Dragonborn**

All Dragonborn gain two features, determined by their subrace. 

The first is resistance to a specific element. This improves to immunity at character level 9\.

The second is an Elemental Breath Cantrip. This Breath Cantrip costs an Action to use, and rolls an Attack Roll against all targets in a 6m/20ft Cone. Successful Attack Rolls deal 1d12 elemental damage and inflict a specific condition for 1 turn. At character level 5 this improves to 2d12 elemental damage and 3 turns for the condition. At character level 9 it further improves to 3d12+12 elemental damage. Your Spellcasting Attribute is determined by the class in which you have the most levels, as shown on your Character Sheet with a Star above that Attribute. 

**Black Dragonborn:** Gain Resistance to Acid damage and a Breath Cantrip that deals Acid elemental damage and inflicts the Acid condition. 

**Blue Dragonborn:** Gain Resistance to Lightning damage and a Breath Cantrip that deals Lightning elemental damage and inflicts the Shocked condition. 

**Brass Dragonborn:** Gain Resistance to Fire damage and a Breath Cantrip that deals Fire elemental damage and inflicts the Burning condition. 

**Bronze Dragonborn:** Gain Resistance to Lightning damage and a Breath Cantrip that deals Lightning elemental damage and inflicts the Shocked condition. 

**Copper Dragonborn:** Gain Resistance to Acid damage and a Breath Cantrip that deals Acid elemental damage and inflicts the Acid condition. 

**Gold Dragonborn:** Gain Resistance to Fire damage and a Breath Cantrip that deals Fire elemental damage and inflicts the Burning condition.  

**Green Dragonborn:** Gain Resistance to Poison damage and a Breath Cantrip that deals Poison elemental damage and inflicts the Poisoned condition. 

**Red Dragonborn:** Gain Resistance to Fire damage and a Breath Cantrip that deals Fire elemental damage and inflicts the Burning condition. 

**Silver Dragonborn:** Gain Resistance to Cold damage and a Breath Cantrip that deals Cold elemental damage and inflicts the Frostbite condition. 

**White Dragonborn:** Gain Resistance to Cold damage and a Breath Cantrip that deals Cold elemental damage and inflicts the Frostbite condition. 

**Amethyst Dragonborn:** Gain Resistance to Force damage and a Breath Cantrip that deals Force elemental damage and inflicts the Dazed condition. 

**Crystal Dragonborn:** Gain Resistance to Radiant damage and a Breath Cantrip that deals Radiant elemental damage and inflicts the Blind condition. 

**Emerald Dragonborn:** Gain Resistance to Psychic damage and a Breath Cantrip that deals Psychic elemental damage and inflicts the Mental Fatigue condition. 

**Sapphire Dragonborn:** Gain Resistance to Thunder damage and a Breath Cantrip that deals Thunder elemental damage and inflicts the Reverberation condition. 

**Topaz Dragonborn:** Gain Resistance to Necrotic damage and a Breath Cantrip that deals Radiant elemental damage and inflicts the Bone Chilled condition. 

## **Drow**

All Drow gain Superior Darkvision, granting them the ability to see in magical darkness. Additionally, while shrouded in total darkness they make all Attack Rolls with Advantage.

**Lolth-Sworn Drow:** Cast Silence as an Action once per combat. 

**Seldarine Drow:** Cast Misty Step as a Bonus Action once per combat. 

## **Dwarf**

**Gold Dwarf:** Gain a \+2 bonus to all damage dealt with weapons. *(Does not include unarmed attacks.)*

**Shield Dwarf:** Reduce all damage taken by 2\. *(Calculated after Resistances and stacks with all other sources of Damage Reduction.)*

**Duergar:** Cast Enlarge on yourself as a Bonus Action once per combat. Duergar gain Superior Darkvision, granting them the ability to see in magical darkness. Additionally, while shrouded in total darkness they make all Attack Rolls with Advantage.

## **Elf**

**High Elf:** Gain Advantage on all Wisdom based Ability Checks and Saving Throws.

**Wood Elf:** Movement speed is increased by 9m/20ft. 

## **Githyanki**

When making any Attack Roll, add \+2 to your attack rolls. Additionally, you are Resistant to Psychic damage and have Basic Darkvision.

## **Gnome**

**Rock Gnome:** Armour Class is increased by 2\.

**Forest Gnome:** Cast Dominate Beast as an Action once per round. Forest Gnomes have the ability to Speak With Animals at all times, and are immune to effects that would Restrain them.

**Deep Gnome:** Cast Invisibility on yourself as a Bonus Action once per combat. Deep Gnomes gain Superior Darkvision, granting them the ability to see in magical darkness. Additionally, while shrouded in total darkness they make all Attack Rolls with Advantage.

## **Half-Elf**

All Half-Elves gain a \+2 bonus to Initiative.

**High Half-Elf:** Gain Advantage on all Wisdom based Saving Throws.

**Wood Half-Elf:** Movement speed is increased by 4,5 m.

**Drow Half-Elf:** Gain Superior Darkvision, granting them the ability to see in magical darkness. Additionally, while shrouded in total darkness they make all Attack Rolls with Advantage.

## **Half-Orc**

Gain a \+2 bonus to Initiative, as well \+2 to STR and Darkvision.

## **Halfling**

**Lightfoot Halfling:** Gain Advantage on all Dexterity based Ability Checks and Saving Throws.

**Strongheart Halfling:** Gain Advantage on all Constitution based Ability Checks and Saving Throws. 

## **Human**

Gain a \+4 bonus to Initiative.

## **Tiefling**

All Tieflings gain Resistance to Fire and Darkvision. Additionally, they gain the ability to cast a specific spell once per combat. This spell scales with character level, being treated as if cast with a higher level spell slot at third level and every odd level thereafter. Your Spellcasting Attribute for this Spell is determined by the class in which you have the most levels, as shown on your Character Sheet with a Star above that Attribute. 

**Asmodeus Tiefling:** Cast Fireball once per combat.

**Mephistopheles Tiefling:** Cast Burning Hands as an Action once per combat.

**Zariel Tiefling:** Cast Fire Shield once per combat.
