## **Class Resource: Wild Shape Charges**

Druids have 3 Wild Shape charges that are replenished on a short rest or long rest. *Wild Shape charges can also be recovered through the Natural Bounty and Wild Stride class passives.*

## **Wild Shape**

▲ **Bonus Action**, Consumes Wild Shape Charge

## You may magically assume the shape of a beast. At first level, you may learn 3 of the 9 available Wild Shape forms. At class level 5 you may learn another 3 forms, and at class level 9 you may learn the final 3 forms. This ability is explained further in the Wild Shape Forms section of this document. 

## **Beastcraft**

**🔄 Toggleable**

This passive allows you to use your Wild Shape as an Action or Bonus Action. This may be toggled at any time.

## **Return to Form**

**🔄 Toggleable**

At the end of combat, immediately return to your natural form and refund one Wild Shape charge. This can be toggled at any time.

## **Cantrips**

At first level you may select two cantrips. Your Spellcasting Ability as a Druid is Wisdom. You may select an additional cantrip at levels 5 and 9\.

## **Spellcasting**

At first level you may select two spells from the Druid spell list. Your Spellcasting Ability as a Druid is Wisdom. At each level gained, you may select two additional spells known. At class levels 3, 7, and 11 you are not limited to the Druid spell list and may instead select two spells from any spell list. At every level after 1st, you may choose to replace a single spell known with a different spell from the Druid list. 

## **Cycle of Balance**

**Passive Feature**

Each turn while in combat, you cycle through one of the four seasons. Each season grants two passive damage buffs equal to your Druid levels divided by 2, as well as damage resistance corresponding to your seasonal damage types.

- **Spring:** Affected creature is attuned to the season of Spring. They deal additional Lightning and Thunder damage equal to their levels in Druid divided by two and are Resistant to those types of damage as well. In addition, they may morph into an Air Myrmidon at any time as a free action.  
    
- **Summer:** Affected creature is attuned to the season of Summer. They deal additional Fire and Radiant damage equal to their levels in Druid divided by two and are Resistant to those types of damage as well. In addition, they may morph into a Fire Myrmidon at any time as a free action.

- **Fall:** Affected creature is attuned to the season of Fall. They deal additional Poison and Necrotic damage equal to their levels in Druid divided by two and are Resistant to those types of damage as well. In addition, they may morph into an Earth Myrmidon at any time as a free action

- **Winter:** Affected creature is attuned to the season of Winter. They deal additional Cold and Force damage equal to their levels in Druid divided by two and are Resistant to those types of damage as well. In addition, they may morph into a Water Myrmidon at any time as a free action.

In addition, each season allows you to morph into a corresponding elemental Myrmidon.

## **Terrasoul**

**Passive Feature**

The first time each turn you deal damage with a spell associated with your seasonal damage types, you gain one use of Seasonal Reprieve.

When you use your action to make an unarmed or weapon-based Attack Roll with your main hand, you may make an additional attack for free.

## **Tempestuous Seasons**

**Passive Feature**

Gain the ability to manually advance your seasons by expanding a Wildshape Charge, dealing damage, within a 9m area. The damage type dealt changes to make the season that you had upon advancing. 

| 7th Level |
| :---- |

**Passive Feature**

In combat, you ignore all forms of damage Resistance and Immunities associated with your current season.

| 9th Level |
| :---- |

**Passive Feature**

## **Maelstrom**

**Passive Feature**

On a killing blow or Critical Hit, you trigger Tempestuous Seasons within a 9m radius around your target.  
Triggering Tempestuous Seasons in this manner will not advance your seasons.

**Passive Feature**

## **Class Resource: Wild Shape Charges**

Druids have 3 Wild Shape charges that are replenished on a short rest or long rest. *Wild Shape charges can also be recovered through the Natural Bounty and Wild Stride class passives.*

## **Wild Shape**

▲ **Bonus Action**, Consumes Wild Shape Charge

## You may magically assume the shape of a beast. At first level, you may learn 3 of the 9 available Wild Shape forms. At class level 5 you may learn another 3 forms, and at class level 9 you may learn the final 3 forms. This ability is explained further in the Wild Shape Forms section of this document. 

## **Beastcraft**

**🔄 Toggleable**

This passive allows you to use your Wild Shape as an Action or Bonus Action. This may be toggled at any time.

## **Return to Form**

**🔄 Toggleable**

At the end of combat, immediately return to your natural form and refund one Wild Shape charge. This can be toggled at any time.

## **Cantrips**

At first level you may select two cantrips. Your Spellcasting Ability as a Druid is Wisdom. You may select an additional cantrip at levels 5 and 9\.

## **Spellcasting**

At first level you may select two spells from the Druid spell list. Your Spellcasting Ability as a Druid is Wisdom. At each level gained, you may select two additional spells known. At class levels 3, 7, and 11 you are not limited to the Druid spell list and may instead select two spells from any spell list. At every level after 1st, you may choose to replace a single spell known with a different spell from the Druid list. 

## **Class Resource: Star Map**

Circle of the Stars Druids have 3 Star Maps that are replenished on a short rest or long rest. 

## **Resurgence of the Cosmos**

**Free Action (Once a turn),** Consumes Wild Shape Charge

Restore one Star Map.

## **Shape of the Cosmos**

▲ **Bonus Action**

Take on a Shape of the Cosmos, allowing the constellation of a guiding figure to watch over you. Each Shape lasts until Long Rest and enables the use of a unique Cantrip.

* **Archer:** Grants Luminous Arrow (Bonus Action, \[1d12/2d12 at level 5/3d12 at level 9\] Radiant damage to a target within 18m/60ft, consumes 1 Star Map)  
* **Chalice:** Grants Chalice Healing (Bonus action, heals a creature within 18m/60ft \[1d12/2d12 at level 5/3d12 at level 9\] hit points, consumes 1 Star Map)  
* **Dragon:** Grants Breath of the Cosmos (Bonus action, deals \[1d12/2d12 at level 5/3d12 at level 9\] Radiant damage to all targets in a 6m/20ft cone, consumes 1 Star Map)

## **Extra Attack**

When you use your action to make an unarmed or weapon based Attack Roll with your main hand, you may make an additional attack for free.

## **Guidance of the Stars**

**Passive Feature**

Each of the Cantrips provided by your Shape of the Cosmos is replaced by a new and stronger Cantrip. 

* **Archer:** Grants Luminous Barrage (Bonus Action, 2d12 Radiant damage to a target within 18m/60ft, fires 3 arrows, consumes 1 Star Map)  
* **Chalice:** Grants Ritual Chalice (Bonus Action, creates a 3m/10ft radius area of healing within 18m/60ft that restores 2d12 hit points to all creatures, consumes 1 Star Map)  
* **Dragon:** Grants Solar Flare (Bonus Action, deals 2d12 Radiant damage to all creatures in a 18m/60ft line, consumes 1 Star Map)

## **Class Resource: Cosmic Omen**

Circle of the Stars Druids have 3 Cosmic Omens that are replenished on a short rest or long rest. Each time you long rest, you experience a prophecy and gain access to either the Omen of Weal or Omen of Woe, chosen randomly. 

## **Cosmic Omen of Weal: Attack Roll**

**▢** **Free Reaction,** Consumes a Cosmic Omen, Requires Omen of Weal

Add \+1d4 bonus to an ally’s Attack Roll if it would otherwise miss.

## **Cosmic Omen of Weal: Saving Throw**

**▢** **Free Reaction,** Consumes a Cosmic Omen, Requires Omen of Weal

Add \+1d4 bonus to an ally’s Saving Throw if it would otherwise fail.

## **Cosmic Omen: Ability Check**

**▢** **Free Action,** Consumes a Cosmic Omen, Requires Omen of Weal

Add \+1d4 bonus to an ally’s Ability Checks for 1 turn.

## **Cosmic Omen of Woe: Attack Roll**

**▢** **Free Reaction,** Consumes a Cosmic Omen, Requires Omen of Woe

Subtract \-1d4 from an enemy’s Attack Roll if it would otherwise hit.

## **Cosmic Omen of Woe: Saving Throw**

**▢** **Free Reaction,** Consumes a Cosmic Omen, Requires Omen of Woe

Subtract \-1d4 from an enemy’s Saving Throw if it would otherwise succeed.

## **Twinkling Constellations**

**Passive Feature**

# **Wild Shape Forms**

Assuming a Wild Shape replaces your base Strength, Dexterity, and Constitution score as well as your basic attack. Actions are otherwise limited by your form. You are still considered to be wearing all of your equipment. Your Armour Class and damage reduction are calculated based on the Armour you are wearing. Your physical stats will be modified by all relevant effects, including gear and the Ability Improvement feat. Modifications to your Constitution score do not affect the amount of Hit Points a form has, but will still affect Saving Throws. You still have full access to your inventory and can even use consumables, but cannot change any worn equipment. 

Attacks in Wild Shape forms use the higher of Strength or Dexterity when determining your Attack Roll and damage. Cat and Dire Raven forms do not add their attack attribute to damage rolls. The Myrmidon (Air) always uses Strength due to the fact that its weapon is a Flail and lacks the Finesse property. 

*Engine Note: If you enter a Myrmadon (Air, Fire, or Water) Wild Shape while wielding a one handed or versatile weapon in your main hand and nothing in your off hand, your weapon may end up in your off hand when you take another Wild Shape or return to your caster form. If you enter a Wild Shape form with an empty main hand and a weapon in your off hand, the engine will not assign your form’s Basic Attack to the action slot. The Basic Attack will still appear on your action bar and can be used manually. To resolve this issue, open your character sheet and move your weapon back to your main hand.*

# **Druidic Roots (Passives)**

A character may select two passive abilities at levels 2, 6, and 10\. Certain passive abilities also appear on Rare quality equipment found throughout your adventures. 

*\*Also affect your summons (for the Druid specifically, most passives only affect your Dryad).*  
---

## **Armour of Thorns\***

When a creature damages you with a melee based Attack Roll, deal Piercing damage to them equal to your Wisdom Modifier. 

---

## **Combat’s Harvest**

On your first turn of any combat encounter, cast Goodberry. *(As a first level spell targeting yourself.)* Your goodberries from all sources restore significantly more heal points.

---

## **Earthen Sentinel**

When you roll a Critical hit with a Spell, Petrify your target for one turn.

---

## **Feral Precision\***

Gain a \+2 bonus to all Attack Rolls. This bonus is doubled while assuming a Wild Shape, a Shape of the Cosmos, or while Symbiotic Entity is active. 

---

## **Feral Resilience**

Upon assuming the form of a While Shape, gain Temporary Hit Points equal to your Druid level multiplied by four. *(This only occurs when entering a Wild Shape form from your natural form, not when swapping between forms with Moonbreak.)*

---

## **Ferocious Stand\***

Gain a \+1 bonus to your Armour Class. This bonus is doubled while assuming a Wild Shape, a Shape of the Cosmos, or while Symbiotic Entity is active. 

---

## **Instinctive Defense\***

While assuming a form of a Wild Shape, you gain Resistance to all Physical damage. 

---

## **Killer Instinct\***

Gain advantage on all damage rolls while assuming the form of a Wild Shape. 

---

## **Mantra of Restoration**

While not assuming the form of a Wild Shape, you may expend Wild Shape charges with Solar Mend in order to restore Spell Slots (**▢** Free Action). Additionally, gain Lunar Mend, allowing you to heal yourself while in the form of a Wild Shape.

---

## **Natural Bounty\***

Once per turn, upon landing a killing blow or rolling a Critical Hit on an enemy, restore one of your Wild Shape charges. 

---

## **Natural Resurgence\***

While in combat, regenerate Hit Points each turn equal to your Wisdom Modifier. This passive has no effect while Downed. 

---

## **Nature’s Mercy\***

Gain Resistance to Cold, Lightning and Poison damage.

---

## **Nature’s Wrath\***

Use your Wild Shape charges to deal an additional \+1d12 Cold, Lightning, or Poison damage on any successful Attack Roll made with a weapon. 

---

## **Pack Leader\***

Damaging other creatures potentially Goads them into attacking you. (*Challenged enemies have \-5 to their attack roll when they are not attacking you. The AI will try to focus you.*)

---

## **Primal Bloodletter\***

While assuming the form of a Wild Shape, a Shape of the Cosmos, or while Symbiotic Entity is active, you have a chance to inflict Gaping Wounds on your target when you make a successful Attack Roll. 

---

## **Primal Surge\***

Deal additional damage while Wild Shaped equal to your Wisdom Modifier. 

---

## **Primal Takedown\***

While assuming the form of a Wild Shape, a Shape of the Cosmos, or while Symbiotic Entity is active, you have a chance to knock your target Prone when you make a successful Attack Roll. 

---

## **Shapechanger’s Versatility**

Outside of combat, you may Wild Shape as a **▢** Free Action. *(You do not consume Wild Shape charges out of combat when assuming a Wild Shape form.)*

---

## **Territorial Dominance\***

Gain Advantage when making an Attack Roll against a target in Difficult Terrain. 

---

## **Wild Stride\***

While in combat, regenerate one Wild Shape charge every three turns.
