## **Class Resource: Channel Divinity**

Clerics have 3 Channel Divinity charges that are replenished on a short rest or long rest. *Channel Divinity charges can also be recovered through the Paradisiacal Gift class passive.*

## **Channel Divinity: Pacify Undead**

⬤ **Action,** Consumes Channel Divinity charge

All undead within 9m/30ft are Pacified for 1 turn on a failed Will Save. Pacified undead cannot act, and attacks made against them while within 3m/10ft are always Critical Hits.

## **Class Resource: Divine Intervention**

During combat, at the start of each turn the Cleric rolls for a chance to obtain their Deity’s favor. The cleric has a 33% chance to gain a charge of Divine Intervention. When you gain a charge of Divine Intervention you will see a shaft of light briefly appear over the Cleric, and a golden ring will encircle the Cleric’s feet until consumed. A Cleric can have no more than one Divine Intervention charge at any time, and will lose a held charge when combat ends.

You can also use an ⬤ **Action** and a Channel Divinity charge to trigger a Miracle during your turn.

## **Divine Miracles**

**▢** **Free Action**

This feature allows the Cleric to expend a Divine Intervention charge to perform powerful miracles. The Divine Miracles ability will change color from dim to bright when you have a Divine Intervention charge. Each of the four abilities within this feature is a free action and consumes your Divine Intervention charge. 

* **Arcane Bounty:** Recover one expended Spell Slot equal to the highest level you are capable of casting with Cleric levels. *(This ability only uses Cleric levels to determine your highest level Spell Slot. It will not restore lower spell slots, even if those are your only expended slots. It will also not restore higher spell slots if you have gained them from another spellcasting class.)*  
* **Deific Necrosis:** Deal 1d6 Necrotic damage to all enemies within 9m/30ft on a failed Wisdom Save. Enemies take half damage on a successful save. Any enemies suffering from Plague of Rot will receive an additional stack. Damage improves to 2d6 at Cleric level 3, with an additional 1d6 every odd level thereafter.  
* **Holy Restoration:** Restore 1d6 Hit Points to yourself and all allies within 9m/30ft. This healing improves to 2d6 at Cleric level 3, with an additional 1d6 every odd level thereafter.  
* **Wreathed in Radiance:** Grant yourself and all allies within 9m/30ft a bonus to Attack Rolls equal to half your cleric level (rounded up) for 3 turns.

## **Cantrips**

At first level you may select two cantrips. Your Spellcasting Ability as a Cleric is Wisdom. You may select an additional cantrip at levels 5 and 9\.

## **Spellcasting**

At first level you may select two spells from the Cleric spell list. Your Spellcasting Ability as a Cleric is Wisdom. At each level gained, you may select two additional spells known. At class levels 3, 7, and 11 you are not limited to the Cleric spell list and may instead select two spells from any spell list. At every level after 1st, you may choose to replace a single spell known with a different spell from the Cleric list. 

## **Bursting Sinew**

⬤ **Action,** Necromancy Cantrip

Target a corpse or undead creature within 18m/60ft, detonating it and dealing 1d8 Necrotic damage to all creatures in a 3m/10ft radius on a failed Constitution Save. Creatures take half damage on a successful save. If this detonation hits another corpse or undead creature, it will cause that target to detonate and deal the same amount of damage in a 3m/10ft radius. Any creature damaged will receive one stack of **Plague of Rot** per detonation that struck them. Damage for Bursting Sinew increases to 2d8 at level 5, and 3d8 at level 9\.

## **Plague of Rot**

**Condition**

Creatures with Plague of Rot will take (prof) Necrotic damage for each stack of the condition at the beginning of each turn in combat. At the end of each turn, they will gain an additional stack of Plague of Rot. This condition has an infinite duration, but will only deal damage and accumulate stacks while in combat. Plague of Rot is considered to be the Poisoned condition for purposes of removal/healing, and is applied by Bursting Sinew.

## **Channel Divinity: Curse of Undeath**

▲ **Bonus Action,** Consumes Channel Divinity

Target creature within 18m/60ft is afflicted with the Curse of Rot. They are considered to be Undead for the purposes of spells and abilities, and gain immunity to Necrotic and Poison damage. Curse of Undeath lasts until Long Rest or Concentration is interrupted.

## **Extra Attack**

## **Insurmountable Suffering**

**Passive Feature**

When damaging enemies, you ignore all forms of Resistance and Immunities to Necrotic damage. *(Targets with Curse of Undeath remain immune to Necrotic damage and will not gain stacks of Plague of Rot from Bursting Sinew.)*

## **Harvest**

**🔄 Toggleable**

You afflict all corpses within 9m/30ft with Fresh Harvest. Fresh Harvest radiates 3m/10ft and afflicts all enemies with Harvest’s Yield. Harvest’s Yield deals 1d12 Necrotic damage at the end of the creature’s turn and causes them to gain an additional stack of Plague of Rot if they are already afflicted. This ability can be toggled at any time. *(Harvest affects any corpse, including those that have been detonated or consumed by Animate Dead.)*

## **Steel Cast**

**Passive Feature**

## **True Pestilence**

**Passive Feature**

## **Death of the Firstborns**

**Passive Feature**

The first instance of Necrotic damage you deal in a battle causes your target to become Vulnerable to all damage for 1 turn. *(If dealing damage to multiple targets, only one is afflicted with vulnerability.)*

**At level 1,** your hammer deals \+1d4 fire damage, enchanted \+1 and has Heated Firmament: Gain Resistance to Fire Damage.

## **Class Resource: Divine Intervention**

During combat, at the start of each turn the Cleric rolls for a chance to obtain their Deity’s favor. The cleric has a 33% chance to gain a charge of Divine Intervention. ## **Divine Miracles**

**▢** **Free Action**

This feature allows the Cleric to expend a Divine Intervention charge to perform powerful miracles. The Divine Miracles ability will change color from dim to bright when you have a Divine Intervention charge. Each of the four abilities within this feature is a free action and consumes your Divine Intervention charge.

* **Holy Restoration:** Restore 1d6 Hit Points to yourself and all allies within 9m/30ft. This healing improves to 2d6 at Cleric level 3, with an additional 1d6 every odd level thereafter.  
* **Wreathed in Radiance:** Grant yourself and all allies within 9m/30ft a bonus to Attack Rolls equal to half your cleric level (rounded up) for 3 turns.

A character may select two passive abilities at levels 2, 6, and 10\. Certain passive abilities also appear on Rare quality equipment found throughout your adventures. 

*\*Also affect your summons.*  
---

## **Aegis**

Gain Advantage on all Constitution based Saving Throws.

---

## **Blessed Resolve\***

Upon landing a killing blow or Critical Hit, on a non-allied creature, emit an aura which Blesses all allies within 9m/30ft of you for three turns.

---

## **Blood for Blood**

The first time you heal a creature each turn, the number you need in order to roll a Critical Hit is reduced by 1\. This effect will reset upon landing a Critical Hit.

---

## **Consecrative Sacrifice\***

Upon landing a killing blow or Critical Hit on a non-allied creature, emit a healing aura across 9m/30ft which restores \+1d6 Hit Points multiplied by your Proficiency Bonus to yourself and all allies. This effect can trigger once per turn.

---

## **Divine Commune**

Increase your odds of experiencing instances of Divine Intervention from 33% to 50%.

---

## **Divine Restoration**

At the end of any combat encounter, emit a healing aura across 45m/150ft which restores \+1d6 Hit Points multiplied by your proficiency bonus to yourself and all allies.

---

## **Ethereal Intuition\***

Permanently gain the effects of Guidance and Resistance.

---

## **Fell Repentance**

When an enemy makes an Attack Roll against an ally within 9m/30ft, you may use your **✦** Reaction in order to retaliate with Sacred Flame, Bone Chill or Spark Surge.

---

## **Holy Fortitude\***

While below half your total Hit Points, you regain the maximum amount of Hit Points possible when healed.

---

## **Guided Strikes**

Upon healing a creature with a Spell, you gain Advantage on your next Attack Roll.

---

## **Immaculate Retribution**

When an enemy damages you with a melee based Attack roll, deal Radiant/Lightning/Necrotic damage to them equal to your Wisdom Modifier multiplied by 2 (the damage type depends on your subclass).

---

## **Immaculate Ward**

When an enemy makes an Attack Roll against you, you may use your **✦** Reaction and one of your Channel Divinity charges in order to receive Immaculate Blade Ward for one turn. *(Immaculate Blade Ward grants immunity to the next instance of damage.)*

---

## **Martyrdom\***

Upon being Downed or killed, erupt as a raging inferno, uncontrollably scorching everything around you.

---

## **Paradisiacal Gift**

Once per turn, upon casting a Miracle, immediately recover one of your Channel Divinity charges.

---

## **Sacral Touch**

Upon healing a creature, potentially experience an instance of Divine Intervention. This may only occur once per turn. 

---

## **Sanctified Presence\***

At the beginning of each of your turns in combat, regain Hit Points equal to your Proficiency Bonus multiplied by two.

---

## **Sanctifying Aura**

Emit an Aura across 9m/30ft which provides Sanctuary to allies who are Downed.

---

## **Stalwart**

Permanently gain the effects of Dispel Evil and Good. 

---

## **Stout Believer**

Gain status immunity to all forms of fear. Additionally, upon healing a creature with a Spell, they receive Death Ward for three turns.

---

## **Venerational Strikes**

Upon casting a Miracle, receive Veneration for three turns. *(Weapons deal additional Radiant/lightning/necrotic damage equal to your Wisdom modifier.)*
