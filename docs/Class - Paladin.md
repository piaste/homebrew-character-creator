## **Class Resource: Channel Oath Charge**

Paladins have 3 Channel Oath charges that are replenished on a short rest or long rest. *Channel Oath charges can also be restored through the Atonement, Critical Oath, Oathbound Renewal, and Smite Makes Right class passives.*

## **Class Resource: Crusader’s Smite**

## Paladins have 3 Crusader’s Smite charges that are replenished on a short rest or long rest. *Crusader’s Smite charges can also be restored through the Atonement, Death Sentence, and Divine Resurgence class passives, as well as the Ancient Judgement class feature.*

## **Cantrips**

At first level you may select two cantrips. Your Spellcasting Ability as a Paladin is Charisma. You may select an additional cantrip at levels 5 and 9\.

## **Spellcasting**

## At first level you may select one spell from the Cleric spell list. Your Spellcasting Ability as a Paladin is Charisma. At each level gained, you may select an additional spell known. At class levels 3, 7, and 11 you are not limited to the Cleric spell list and may instead select a single spell from any spell list. At every level after 1st, you may choose to replace a single spell known with a different spell from the Cleric list. 

## **Smites**

⬤ **Action,** Consumes Crusader’s Smite charge

All Smites are melee OR ranged weapon attacks that add an additional \+1d12 damage determined by the Smite used. Each Smite also applies a debuff on a failed saving throw. The paladin begins with Divine Smite and their choice of 1 additional smite. The paladin may select an additional Smite at 3rd level and every odd level thereafter. The damage of all smites increases by \+1d12 damage at 3rd level and every odd level thereafter. 

* **Divine Smite:** (Default Smite) Radiant damage, inflicts Divine Judgement for 1 turn (all Attack Rolls are made with Advantage against the target)  
* **Blinding Smite:** Radiant damage, inflicts Blind for 3 turns  
* **Branding Smite:** Radiant damage, prevents your target from using their BA for 3 turns.  
* **Searing Smite:** Fire damage, inflicts Searing for 3 turns (1d12 Fire damage)  
* **Staggering Smite:** Psychic damage, target has Disadvantage on saves for 3 turns.  
* **Thunderous Smite:** Thunder damage, inflicts prone for 1 turn  
* **Wrathful Smite:** Psychic damage, inflicts Frightened for 3 turns

## **Healing Radiance**

⬤ **Action,** Consumes Channel Oath charge

Heal yourself and all allies within 4.5m/15ft for an amount equal to your Paladin Level x4. Adds a condition, Healing Radiance, to all targets for 3 turns. This condition heals the target for an amount equal to your Paladin Level x4 when it expires. *(Has no effect on Undead or Constructs.)*

## **Binding Radiance**

▲ **Bonus Action,** Consumes Channel Oath charge

Restrain an enemy within 18m/60ft for 3 turns on a failed Strength save. Restrained targets cannot move and make all Dexterity based Saving Throws with Disadvantage. Attacks against a restrained target are made with Advantage.

## **Ancient Judgement**

**Passive Feature**

When making an Attack Roll or dealing damage to a Fey type creature, roll with Advantage. Once per turn, upon landing a killing blow on a Fey enemy, restore one Crusader’s Smite charge.

| 3rd Level |
| :---- |

## **Soothing Radiance**

**Passive Feature**

Your Healing Radiance now provides the effects of Latent Restoration (cure target of blindness, disease, intoxication, paralysis, and/or poison). Targets of Binding Radiance make their Saving Throw to resist with Disadvantage.

| 5th Level |
| :---- |

## **Extra Attack**

When you use your action to make an unarmed or weapon based Attack Roll with your main hand, you may make an additional attack for free.

## **Turn the Faithless**

⬤ **Action,** Consumes Channel Oath charge

Terrify all creatures in a 9m radius and deal 1d6 radiant damage per 2 Paladin levels. Feys suffer double the damage.

| 7th Level |
| :---- |

## **Extended Oath**

**Passive Feature**

The range of your Healing Radiance is doubled. Additionally, Binding Radiance may now select up to three targets.

| 9th Level |
| :---- |

## **Extra Attack: Martial Surge**

Extra Attack now provides an additional Action at all times, rather than a free attack.

## **Smitten**

**Passive Feature**

When making an Attack Roll with one of your Smites, you make your roll with a \+20 bonus.

## **Aura of Protection**

⬤ **Action,** Duration: Until Long Rest

Emit an aura across 9m/30ft which grants yourself and allies Advantage on Saving Throws.

| 11th Level |
| :---- |

## **Oathbound Defender**

**Passive Feature**

Your Healing Radiance now grants Resistance to all damage until it is recast. When dealing damage to a target affected by your Binding Radiance, all damage dice are rolled with Advantage.

## **Class Resource: Channel Oath Charge**

Paladins have 3 Channel Oath charges that are replenished on a short rest or long rest. *Channel Oath charges can also be restored through the Atonement, Critical Oath, Oathbound Renewal, and Smite Makes Right class passives.*

## **Class Resource: Crusader’s Smite**

## Paladins have 3 Crusader’s Smite charges that are replenished on a short rest or long rest. *Crusader’s Smite charges can also be restored through the Atonement, Death Sentence, and Divine Resurgence class passives, as well as the Righteous Judgement class feature.*

## **Cantrips**

At first level you may select two cantrips. Your Spellcasting Ability as a Paladin is Charisma. You may select an additional cantrip at levels 5 and 9\.

## **Spellcasting**

## At first level you may select one spell from the Cleric spell list. Your Spellcasting Ability as a Paladin is Charisma. At each level gained, you may select an additional spell known. At class levels 3, 7, and 11 you are not limited to the Cleric spell list and may instead select a single spell from any spell list. At every level after 1st, you may choose to replace a single spell known with a different spell from the Cleric list. 

## **Smites**

⬤ **Action,** Consumes Crusader’s Smite charge

All Smites are melee OR Ranged weapon attacks that add an additional \+1d12 damage determined by the Smite used. Each Smite also applies a debuff on a failed saving throw. The paladin begins with Oathbroken Smite and their choice of 1 additional smite. The paladin may select an additional Smite at 3rd level and every odd level thereafter. The damage of all smites increases by \+1d12 damage at 3rd level and every odd level thereafter. 

* **Oathbroken Smite:** (Default Smite) Necrotic damage, inflicts Subjugation on the target.   
* **Blinding Smite:** Radiant damage, inflicts Blind for 3 turns  
* **Branding Smite:** Radiant damage, prevents your target from using their BA for 3 turns.  
* **Searing Smite:** Fire damage, inflicts Searing for 3 turns (1d12 Fire damage)  
* **Staggering Smite:** Psychic damage, target has Disadvantage on saves for 3 turns.  
* **Thunderous Smite:** Thunder damage, inflicts prone for 1 turn  
* **Wrathful Smite:** Psychic damage, inflicts Frightened for 3 turns

## **Subjugate Creature**

⬤ **Action,** Consumes Channel Oath charge

Until long rest, the creature claimed by the oathbreaker will attack its allies.

## **Spiteful Suffering**

▲ **Bonus Action,** Consumes Channel Oath charge

Until Long Rest, submit your target to a malicious onslaught of spite, filling them with a sense of imposing dread. All **Attack Rolls** made against them will roll with **Advantage**. Upon dying, your target's dread will then manifest as a shadow under your command.

#  **Refined Adjudications (Passives)**

A character may select two passive abilities at levels 2, 6, and 10\. Certain passive abilities also appear on Rare quality equipment found throughout your adventures.   
*\*Also affect your summons.*  
---

## **Atonement**

Upon expending one of your Channel Oath charges, you have a 33% chance to recover one of your Crusader’s Smite charges. Additionally, upon expending one of your Crusader’s Smite charges, you have a 33% chance to recover one of your Channel Oath charges.  
---

**Critical Oath**  
Once per turn, upon landing a Critical Hit, restore one Channel Oath charge.  
---

**Death Sentence**  
Each turn, you have a 33% chance to recover a Crusader's Smite charge.  
---

**Divine Health\***  
Gain Resistance to Poison and Necrotic damage. You also heal for 1d6 x proficiency bonus when you kill an enemy.  
---

**Divine Resurgence**  
While in combat, restore one Crusader Smite charge every three turns.  
---

**Divine Sense\***  
Gain Advantage on Attack Rolls against Fey, Fiends, Undeads and Aberrations.  
---

**Divine Shielding\***  
When taking any damage, gain Resistance to that type of damage for 3 rounds. This effect stack.  
---

**Holy Smite**  
When you deal damage with Smite, you heal every ally (including yourself) in a 3m/10ft radius for the value of your Smite’s damage.  
---

**Holy Bulwark**  
When you deal damage with Smite, gain Divine Favor and Shield of Faith for three turns.  
---

**Infaillible Beacon**  
At the start of your turn, you Challenge all enemies within 9m (*Challenged enemies have \-5 to their attack roll when they are not attacking you. The AI will try to focus you.*)  
---

**Lay on Hands**  
Use a Crusader Smite charge to Heal yourself or allies, or to cure poisons and most conditions. (*1d12 HP/2 lvl,* **▢** *Free Action, cost a Channel Oath charge, melee range*)  
---

**Oathbound Renewal**  
While in combat, restore one Channel Oath charge every three turns.  
---

**Oath Channeling**  
Spend your Channel Oath charges to restore Spell Slots. (**▢** *Free action once per turn, 1 Channel Oath Charge for 1 spell slot*)  
---

**Principle of Belief\***  
When dealing damage to Fey, Fiends, Undeads or Aberrations, you deal an additional 1d12 damage. *(Separate damage rider, damage type is the same as your initial source of damage).*  
---

**Repel the Damned\***  
All enemies within 9m/30ft take Radiant damage each turn equal to your Charisma Modifier. Damage type changes based on subclass  
---

**Resplendent Reverb**  
Healing an entity with a Spell immediately heals yourself for a value equal to your Charisma Modifier x your proficiency bonus.  
---

**Smite Makes Right**  
Once per turn, when killing an enemy with a Smite, restore one Channel Oath charge.  
---

**Stalwart Eruption**  
When killing an enemy with a Smite, gain Heroism for three turns.  
---

**Stalwart Resolve\***  
Decrease elemental damage you take by your Charisma modifier.  
---

**Visage of Sanctity**  
Gain a bonus to your Ability Checks equal to your Charisma Modifier.
