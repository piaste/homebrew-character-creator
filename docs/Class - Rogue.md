## **Class Resource: Luck Points**

Rogues have 3 Luck Points that are replenished on a short rest or long rest. Luck Points are primarily used to give yourself Advantage or force an enemy to redo their roll. *Luck Points can also be recovered through the Manipulator class passive.*

## **Lucky Odds: Attack Rolls**

**🔄 Toggleable**

Spend one of your Lucky dice to gain Advantage on Attack Rolls or force Enemies to Attack you with Disadvantage. Once per turn, the first time you deal or take damage will consume one Luck Point. 

## **Lucky Odds: Ability Checks**

**🔄 Toggleable**

Spend one of your Lucky dice to gain Advantage on your next Ability Check. This effect can be toggled at any time.

## **Nimble Movement**

**🔄 Toggleable**

Gain the ability to Dash, Disengage, and Hide as a Bonus Action. This effect can be toggled at any time.

## **Arcane Sneak Attack**

**▢** **Free Reaction**  
Once per turn, deal an extra 1d6 Piercing damage to a creature within 1.5m/5ft of an ally, or against one you have Advantage against. If casting a Cantrip or Spell, Sneak Attack will deal 1d6 Force Damage. You must be wielding a Finesse Weapon in order to trigger Sneak Attack.

## **Cantrips**

At first level you may select two cantrips. Your Spellcasting Ability as a Rogue is Intelligence. You may select an additional cantrip at levels 5 and 9\.

## **Spellcasting**

At first level you may select one spell from the Wizard spell list. Your Spellcasting Ability as a Rogue is Intelligence. At each level gained, you may select an additional spell known. At class levels 3, 7, and 11 you are not limited to the Wizard spell list and may instead select a single spell from any spell list. At every level after 1st, you may choose to replace a single spell known with a different spell from the Wizard list. 

## **Arcane Trickery**

Casting a Spell or Cantrip while Hidden will not break Stealth.

Once per turn, deal an extra 1d6 Piercing damage to a creature within 1.5m/5ft of an ally, or against one you have Advantage against. You must be wielding a Finesse Weapon in order to trigger Sneak Attack.

## **Get Out of Jail Free**

**Passive Feature**

You always start combat with all of your Luck Points available. 

## **Get Out of Jail Free (Hit)**

**▢** **Free Reaction,** Consumes Luck Point

When an enemy is about to hit you, use a Luck Point in order to cause it to miss.

## **Get Out of Jail Free (Miss)**

**▢** **Free Reaction,** Consumes Luck Point

When you are about to miss an enemy, you can use a Luck Point in order to cause it to hit.
