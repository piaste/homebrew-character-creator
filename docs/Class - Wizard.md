## **Class Resource: Arcane Recovery Charge**

Wizards have a maximum of 1 Arcane Recovery Charge per level in the class. These charges are refilled on a Short or Long Rest. 

## **Arcane Channeling**

**▢** **Free Action,** Consumes Arcane Recovery Charges (varies), Recharge: Per Turn

Recover an expended Spell Slot, up to the maximum level provided by your levels in Wizard. This ability costs 1 Arcane Recovery Charge per level of the Spell Slot recovered. 

## **Scribing**

**Passive Feature**

You can consume scrolls to permanently learn Spells or Cantrips. You can learn any Spell this way, even spells that are not on the Wizard’s spell list.

## **Scholarly Acumen**

**Passive Feature**

Each turn in combat, the wizard will be given a directive to accomplish a specific magical task. This will either be to deal specific type of elemental damage or to cast a Cantrip or Spell from a specific school of magic. Accomplishing this directive will recover one charge of Arcane Recovery. There is no penalty for failing to complete this directive. Tasks are chosen randomly and may include directives that you cannot accomplish with your known cantrips and spells. *(Your current Scholarly Acumen shows up as a condition on your Wizard. Hover over the condition to see the directive.)*

## **Cantrips**

At first level you may select two cantrips. Your Spellcasting Ability as a Wizard is Intelligence. You may select an additional cantrip at levels 5 and 9\.

## **Spellcasting**

At first level you may select two spells from the Wizard spell list. Your Spellcasting Ability as a Wizard is Intelligence. At each level gained, you may select two additional spells known. At class levels 3, 7, and 11 you are not limited to the Wizard spell list and may instead select two spells from any spell list. At every level after 1st, you may choose to replace a single spell known with a different spell from the Wizard list. 

## **Siphon Arcana**

**▢** **Free Action,** Recharge: Per Turn

Target all corpses in a 3m radius around you and destroy them, restoring one Arcane Recovery Charge per corpse and granting you Arcane Force for 3 turns. Arcane Force causes your weapon to deal an additional 1d4 Force damage.

| 3rd Level |
| :---- |

## **Steel-Wind Storm**

▲ **Bonus Action,** Consumes Arcane Recovery Charge

Invoke the Arcane Winds which guide you, granting a \+1 bonus to your Armour Class and Advantage on all melee based Attack Rolls for three turns.

## **Steel-Wind Burst**

⬤ **Action,** Evocation Cantrip

This cantrip targets an enemy within 18m/60ft and deals damage equal to your mainhand melee weapon on a successful Attack Roll. *(This cantrip utilizes your melee weapon’s attack roll and gains all associated benefits for being both a weapon attack and a cantrip.)*

## **Steel-Wind Strike**

⬤ **Action,** Evocation Spell, Consumes a Spell Slot

While not Threatened, teleport to a nearby creature and strike them with your equipped melee weapon. Upcasting this spell will allow you to pick an additional target per level. Multiple targets will be attacked in the order they were selected and you will finish your action in melee range of your final target. *(This spell utilizes your melee weapon’s attack roll and gains all associated benefits for being both a melee weapon attack and a spell.)*

| 5th Level |
| :---- |

**EXTRA ATTACK**  
When you use your action to make an unarmed or weapon based Attack Roll with your main hand, you may make an additional attack for free.

| 7th Level |
| :---- |

## **Winds of the Arcblade**

**Passive Feature**

Once per turn, upon landing a Killing Blow or a Critical Hit, you can cast Steel-Wind Strike as a free action.

| 9th Level |
| :---- |

## **Steel Cast**

**Passive Feature**

Casting a Spell will activate Extra Attack.

| 11th Level |
| :---- |

## **A Storm of Swords**

**Passive Feature**

Upon casting Steel-Wind Strike, a Cloak of Daggers (as the Spell) is created under each of your targets for three turns. The strength of your Cloak of Daggers changes depending on the level of Spell Slot used.

## **Arcane Ward**

**Passive Feature**

Arcane Wardens have the ability to generate and maintain an Arcane Ward. As a free reaction, the Wizard can choose to have the ward absorb an amount of damage equal to the ward's remaining charges. This reduction takes place after calculating Resistance and damage reduction provided by armour. Afterwards, the ward loses one charge.

The first Spell cast each turn adds a number of charges to the Arcane Ward equal to the Spell Slot used. The maximum amount of ward charges you may have at any time is equal to your levels in Wizard.

While you have an Arcane Ward active, enemies are more likely to target you.

## **Elemental Concierge**

**Passive Feature**

Upon dealing elemental damage to an enemy with a Spell or a Cantrip, you generate one Elemental Stain, depending on the damage dealt. Elemental Stain stacks until they are consumed. Can be toggled at any time.

Elemental Stains empower your Spells and Cantrips in various ways :

* **Acid :** The next Spell or Cantrip you cast will reduce your target’s AC by 2 for 1 turn.  
* **Cold :** The next Spell or Cantrip you cast will halve your target’s movement speed for 1 turn.  
* **Fire :** The next Spell or Cantrip you cast will reduce the number required to roll a Critical Hit by 1\.  
* **Force :** The next Spell or Cantrip you cast will ignore all forms of damage Resistance.  
* **Lightning :** The next Spell or Cantrip you cast will prevent your target from using Reactions until the end of their next turn.  
* **Necrotic :** The next Spell or Cantrip you cast will prevent your target from regaining Hit Points for 1 turn.  
* **Poison :** The next Spell or Cantrip will impose Disadvantage on your target’s Attack Rolls until the end of their next turn.  
* **Psychic :** The next Spell or Cantrip you cast will impose Disadvantage on your target’s Saving Throws for 1 turn.  
* **Radiant :** The next Spell or Cantrip you cast will heal an allied target. The amount healed scales with your character level.  
* **Thunder :** The next Spell or Cantrip you cast will Mute your target for 1 turn.

# **Arcane Threads (Passives)**

A character may select two passive abilities at levels 2, 6, and 10\. Certain passive abilities also appear on Rare quality equipment found throughout your adventures.   
*\*Also affect your summons.*  
---

## **Arcane Interruption**

Gain the ability to interrupt any Spell by utilizing your **✦** Reaction and an Arcane Recovery Charge.   
---

## **Arcane Reverb\***

When damaged by a melee based Attack Roll, release a shockwave which forces nearby enemies back by 9m/30ft if they fail a save.   
---

## **Arcane Shield**

While Concentrating, gain Resistance to Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing damage.  
---

## **Boon of Plumes\***

Emit an Aura across 9m/30ft granting both you and allies the effects of Featherfall. If any ally moves out of range of the aura, the effects will persist for one turn.  
---

## **Edict of Divinity**

After each Long Rest, gain three random Portent Dice. During the day, you can use your **✦** Reaction and a charge of your Arcane Recovery to change the die of any Attack Roll or Saving Throw rolled near you to that of one of your Portent Dice. Each Portent Die can only be used once, and you lose your unused Potent Dice at the end of the day. Upon taking a Short Rest you will receive Prophecies for any spent Portent Dice. Complete these Prophecies to regain them.  
---

## **Elemental Countercharge**

When an enemy fails a Melee Weapon Attack against you, use your **✦** Reaction in order to retaliate with Shocking Grasp.  
---

## **Enchanted Safeguard**

Gain the ability to cast the Shield spell (**✦** Reaction, \+4 AC for a round) by expending a charge of your Arcane Recovery.  
---

## **Flames Riposte**

When an enemy fails an Attack Roll against you, use your **✦** Reaction in order to retaliate with Firebolt.  
---

## **Illusory Phantasm\***

On your first turn of any combat encounter, immediately receive the effects of Mirror Image.   
---

## **Magical Insight**

Gain Advantage on Intelligence based Ability Checks and Saving Throws. Additionally, you add your Intelligence Modifier to all Charisma based Skill Checks.  
---

## **Mystic Override\***

You ignore elemental Resistances when dealing damage.  
---

## **Potent Cantrips\***

When dealing damage with a Cantrip, deal additional damage equal to your Proficiency Bonus multiplied by two.  
---

## **Potent Spells\***

Spells you cast gain a damage bonus equal to 4 multiplied by the level of Spell Slot used.  
---

## **Spellblade**

Gain the ability to create an Arcane Bond between you and your weapons, allowing you to use your Spellcasting Ability Modifier for Attack Rolls and damage, as well as restoring one charge of Arcane Recovery when you deal a killing blow or Critical Hit with the weapon.   
---

## **Spellbound Rebirth**

Upon landing a killing blow or Critical Hit on an enemy, restore one charge of Arcane Recovery. This effect can trigger once per turn.  
---

## **Spell Surge**

While in combat, regenerate one charge of Arcane Recovery every three turns.  
---

## **Telekinetic Command**

Gain Telekinetic Push and Telekinetic Pull. Once per turn, you may use either of these as a **▢** Free Action. *(Both abilities have a range of 18m/60ft and move a target 9m/30ft on a failed strength save.)*  
---

## **War Magic\***

After making a melee based Attack Roll or casting a Spell, you may cast a single Cantrip as a ▲ Bonus Action. This may be toggled at any time.  
---

 

## **Wizard’s Clarity**

Upon spending Arcane Recovery charges to recover Spell Slots, you gain Advantage on all damage rolls with Spells and Cantrips. The duration that you retain Advantage is equal to the level of Spell Slot recovered.   
---

## **Woven Precision\***

Reduce the number needed to roll a Critical Hit with Spells and Cantrips by 1\.
