## **Class Resource: Superiority Dice (1/level)**

Fighters have a pool of Superiority Dice equal to their level in Fighter. These dice recharge on a Short or Long Rest and are used to fuel the special abilities of the class. When an ability adds your Superiority Dice to damage, that ability deals an additional 1d6 damage. This damage increases to 2d6 at level 3 and by an additional 1d6 every two levels thereafter. *Superiority Dice can also be replenished through the Full Arsenal and Reckless Abandon Fighter Passive abilities.* 

## **Manoeuvres**

Manoeuvres are powerful Weapon Actions that utilize your Superiority Dice. Weapon Actions are considered basic attacks for the purposes of Extra Attack and Improved Extra Attack. A Fighter selects a single Manoeuvre at level 1 and an additional Manoeuvre at every odd level thereafter. 

## **Second Wind**

**Passive Feature**

Every 3 rounds of battle, you restore 1d12/2 fighter level in Hit Points and recover a Superiority Dice at the end of your turn.

## **Focused Weave** 🔄 **Toggleable**

Your basic Ranged Weapon Attacks no longer utilize Attack Rolls. As such, they can no longer miss your targets or roll Critical Hits. While active, your basic Ranged Weapon Attack is considered to be a Cantrip that deals 1d12 Force damage. This damage increases to 2d12 at level 5, and 3d12 at level 9\. In addition, while active, your Elemental Fletchings will fire stronger, alternative arrows that consume Superiority Dice. 

## **Elemental Fletchings**

You learn 3 Elemental Infusions. Each infusion has a standard version and a focused version that can be used while Focused Weave is toggled on. Focused infusions are stronger and consume a Superiority Die to activate. All elemental infusions are considered weapon cantrips, and thus benefit from any bonuses relevant to weapon attack rolls or cantrips. Infusions that deal damage start at 1d6 elemental damage, and then increase to 2d6 at level 5 and 3d6 at level 9\. They deal normal arrow damage on top of that.

* Acidic Infusion  
  * Standard: ⬤ **Action**, Deals Acid damage and leaves an Acid Surface for three turns.  
  * Focused: ⬤ **Action**, Deals Acid Damage and afflicts your target with Corrosion, lowering their AC.  
* Frost Infusion  
  * Standard: ▲ **Bonus Action**, Summons a climbable ice cube. Enemies starting their turn within 3m of the block may lose 50% of their Movement Speed and take 1d12 Cold Damage.  
  * Focused: ⬤ **Action**, Shoots two rays of frost dealing 1d8 cold damage (cantrip scaling) and halves the target’s movement speed.  
* Searing Infusion  
  * Standard: ⬤ **Action**, Deals Fire damage and generates 3 stacks of Heat  
  * Focused: ⬤ **Action**, Deals 1d8 (cantrip scaling) fire damage in a 3m/10ft radius and burns for 3 turns  
* Lightning Infusion  
  * Standard: ⬤ **Action**, Deals Lightning damage in a 9m/30ft line  
  * Focused: ⬤ **Action**, Deals Lightning damage, forks to two other enemies to deal half damage.  
* Necrotic Infusion  
  * Standard: ⬤ **Action**, Heals the target instead of dealing damage.  
  * Focused: ▲ **Bonus Action**, Gives an ally Death Ward for 3 turns.  
* Poisonous Infusion  
  * Standard: ⬤ **Action**, Deals Poison damage and inflicts Poison for 1 turn  
  * Focused: ▲ **Bonus Action**, Deals Poison damage and inflicts Contagion on a 3 turn cooldown  
* Psychic Infusion  
  * Standard: ⬤ **Action**, Deal Psychic damage and inflict Dazed for 1 turn  
  * Focused: ⬤ **Action,** Deal Psychic damage in a 6m/20ft cone  
* Radiant Infusion  
  * Standard: ⬤ **Action**, Deal Radiant damage, dealing double damage to Aberrations, Fey, Fiends, and Undead  
  * Focused: ⬤ **Action**, Apply Sanctuary to an ally for 3 turns  
* Thunderous Infusion  
  * Standard: ⬤ **Action**, Deal Thunder damage and pull your target 9m/30ft  
  * Focused: ⬤ **Action**, Deal Thunder damage and push your target 9m/30ft

## **Arcane Finesse**

You gain the ability to perform a series of teleportation-based manoeuvres. Each ability is a Cantrip and costs a Superiority Dice to activate. 

* Gateway: ▲ **Bonus Action**, Concentration, Create two Arcane Gates, which any creature may then use to teleport between  
* Quiver Port: ▲ **Bonus Action**, Teleport to a location within 9m/30ft  
* Wrong Warp: ▲ **Bonus Action**, Teleport an object or a creature within 18m/60ft to another location within range

When Focused Weave is active, your basic shots now ricochet to two other creatures if within 9m of each other.

Combat Techniques are mechanically identical to the standard weapon actions found across the variety of weapons in the game, with one exception. Unlike standard weapon actions, your Combat Techniques recharge after three turns. You can use any Combat Technique with any weapon type.

## **Class Resource: Superiority Dice (1/level)**

Fighters have a pool of Superiority Dice equal to their level in Fighter. These dice recharge on a Short or Long Rest, and are used to fuel the special abilities of the class. When an ability adds your Superiority Dice to damage, that ability deals an additional 1d6 damage. This damage increases to 2d6 at level 3 and by an additional 1d6 every two levels thereafter. *Superiority Dice can also be replenished through the Full Arsenal and Reckless Abandon Fighter Passive abilities.*

## **Manoeuvres**

Manoeuvres are powerful Weapon Actions that utilize your Superiority Dice. Weapon Actions are considered basic attacks for the purposes of Extra Attack and Improved Extra Attack. A Fighter selects a single Manoeuvre at level 1 and an additional Manoeuvre at every odd lever thereafter.
